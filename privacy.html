What this site does with your data

The Cohen's Kappa calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter into the confusion matrix are never sent to a server, stored in a database, or shared with anyone. They exist only in your browser's memory while the page is open, and are cleared when you close or reload the tab.
