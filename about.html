About Kappa Calc

Kappa Calc is a single-purpose tool: a fast, accurate, free calculator for Cohen's Kappa, the standard statistic for measuring agreement between two raters or two coding methods. It was built after noticing that most existing statistics calculators bury inter-rater reliability inside general-purpose suites, require signups, or don't report a confidence interval alongside the kappa value itself.

Who it's for

Graduate students running content analysis for a thesis, clinical researchers checking agreement between diagnosticians, qualitative researchers coding interview transcripts, and machine-learning teams validating annotation quality — anyone who needs a trustworthy kappa value without installing statistical software or writing code.

How it works

Everything runs client-side in JavaScript, directly in your browser. Your data — your confusion matrix — is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. Closing the tab clears it completely.

Methodology

Calculations follow Cohen's original 1960 formulation of kappa, with the standard error computed using the large-sample approximation described by Fleiss, Cohen & Everitt (1969). Interpretation bands follow Landis & Koch (1977). See the calculator guide for the full formulas.
